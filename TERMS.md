## 5. Security of what you store

- Clips are stored in your browser profile **without encryption**. Anyone
  or anything with access to that profile (another person on the device,
  malware, a synced or backed-up profile) may be able to read them.
- **Do not use the software as a password manager** or as secure storage
  for secrets, financial details or health information. Use the block list,
  auto-redact and "Forget host" to keep sensitive sites and data out of it.
- Encrypted exports are only as safe as the passphrase you choose. If you
  lose the passphrase, the export cannot be recovered.
- Anything you copy out of the software goes onto your system clipboard,
  where other applications can read it.

If you find a security problem, please report it through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Sanjays2402/context-clipboard/issues) without
including anyone's personal data.
